# Project ANTARA MCR — Revised October 21, 2026

Compile `main.tex` with XeLaTeX (twice for references).

Input: MCR_ANTARA.zip and the MCR DATA, ICDs and CAD, BILL OF MATERIALS.xlsx, and PDR Sims uploads.

The main narrative states the accepted user-provided decisions. Appendix L adds the relevant source-backed science-study disposition tables, plots, BOM and newly uncovered remaining decisions. Historical original material in G–J is maintained for provenance and is not the approved baseline.

Evidence limitations: Different scenario epochs and configurations are not pooled; user-supplied DITL output is from a 2000 epoch. The annual RAAN matrix contains an invalid interpretation of SAA-only orbit count and is not presented as a validated count. The science grid only covers modeled domains, not the whole MCR SAA box. The TensorTech ADCS-10m datasheet describes CMG hardware and does not establish the intended RW+MTQ selection. 25 Wh is a planning figure, while ICEPS Type A lists 28.8 Wh. Payload geometry, rate, data rate and science acceptance remain open. Five-year end of orbit is a target, not verified disposal.

Supporting raw CSVs, CAD and full datasheets are not embedded to keep the Overleaf ZIP manageable; only selected plots and summarized entries are included.
